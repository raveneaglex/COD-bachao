COD ka extra paisa bachao

Hum aapka order prepaid karte hain. Aap delivery par sasta saaman lo.

Kaise kaam karta hai
  1. Product ka link aur dono prices daalo (online aur COD)
  2. Bachat dekho, hum sirf 50% service fee lete hain
  3. Order WhatsApp par bhejo, hum prepaid order kar denge
  4. Saaman aapke address par aayega, aap hume paisa doge
Dono prices daalo, bachat yahan dikhegi.

Fee ka rule: bachat ka 50%. Agar bachat nahi hai to is service ki zaroorat nahi.
